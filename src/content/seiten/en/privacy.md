Protecting your data matters to me. This website collects as little as possible: **no cookies, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** Here's what data is processed anyway, and why.

## Controller

Kaan Kaya, Zug, Switzerland
Email: [kaya.kaan.cr@gmail.com](mailto:kaya.kaan.cr@gmail.com)

This policy follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

## Hosting

This website is hosted by **Netlify, Inc.** (USA). When you visit a page, Netlify processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time, the page requested and your browser type, in order to deliver the website and protect it against attacks. This data is not combined with other data. More information can be found in [Netlify's privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/).

## Contact form and email

If you write to me via the contact form or by email, I process your name, email address and message – solely to reply to you. Form submissions are transmitted and stored via **Netlify Forms** and forwarded to me by email. I don't share your data with third parties and delete it as soon as it's no longer needed.

## Browser storage

The website stores a few settings directly in your browser (local storage): your colour mode (light/dark), your best time in the reaction game and your boxing timer settings. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to me. You can delete it in your browser at any time.

## Fonts

All fonts are served directly from this website. There is no connection to Google Fonts or any other external provider.

## External links

Links to other websites (for example Instagram) lead to third-party services, which have their own privacy policies. Clicking a link doesn't transfer any data from this website to that provider, apart from the usual information sent by your browser.

## Your rights

You have the right at any time to request information about your stored data, and to have it corrected or deleted. Just send me an email.
