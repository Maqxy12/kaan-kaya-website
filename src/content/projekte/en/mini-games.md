## The idea

I didn't just want to learn programming – I wanted to build something people can try right away. That's how my first website came to be: a collection of three small games, which I published myself with Netlify.

## The three games

- **Reaction game:** The field is red. After a random delay it turns green – then click as fast as you can. Click too early and you lose the round. Your best time is saved.
- **Accuracy game:** Hit as many targets as possible within 10 seconds. After every hit the target jumps to a new random spot. Your record is saved.
- **Typing speed game:** A random sentence appears and has to be typed as fast as possible. At the end you see how long it took.

## What I learned

- How JavaScript responds to clicks and input (event listeners)
- How to work with randomness and timers (`Math.random`, `setTimeout`)
- How to save records in the browser (`localStorage`)
- How a website goes live – from a file on my computer to an address on the internet
