## The idea

A place of my own on the internet to introduce myself. I built it during my year in England – back then with the goal of passing the Cambridge C1 and the ECDL. I achieved both.

## What's inside

- **About me:** who I am, why I want to become a software developer and what I dream of
- **Projects:** my mini games collection – with all three games playable right on the page
- **Dark mode:** switch between light and dark at the click of a button
- **Contact:** my email address

## What I learned

How to structure content on a page, how navigation works and how to bring existing projects into a new website.

## From v1 to v2

This website is its successor: bilingual, faster, with case studies, a blog and a CV. But without v1, there would be no v2.
