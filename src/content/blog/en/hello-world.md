Every programmer starts with "Hello world". So I will too.

## Who I am

I'm Kaan, I'm from Zug, Switzerland, and I'm an aspiring software engineer. Right now I attend WISS in Zurich, and in 2027 I start my apprenticeship as an application developer at Roche. On the side, I've been boxing since I was 13.

## Why this website?

I want to show what I build and learn – not just in a CV, but in a way you can click and try. That's why you'll find my projects, my skills and even a new version of the reaction game from my very first web project, the [mini games collection](/en/projects/mini-games/).

## A few things to discover

- Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) for the command palette.
- Press the key left of <kbd>1</kbd> for terminal mode – and type `help`.
- Test your reflexes on the home page.

## What's next

On this blog I'll write about my path into IT, about new projects and about what I learn along the way. Check back now and then – or subscribe to the RSS feed.
